FAQs

We already use Web Traxs. How is this different?

Web Traxs, the free tracker Thomas gives suppliers, reports your website and Thomasnet activity in its own dashboard. Attriform puts the source, ad keyword and pages read on each RFQ itself, in the inbox or CRM where the person quoting it will see it. You can keep using both.

Can it tell a Thomasnet RFQ from an IQS, Google Ads or referral RFQ?

Yes. Visits from Thomasnet, IQS and other directories, Google search and referring websites are recognized from the site that sent them. For Google Ads, the campaign and keyword are added too.

Do I have to drop Thomasnet, IQS or Web Traxs?

No. Attriform works alongside them. It’s how you find out which directory, ad or show actually sends RFQs worth quoting, before you renew them in the 2027 budget.

Will file uploads on my RFQ form still work?

Yes. The form looks and works exactly as it does today, including drawing, STEP file and BOM uploads. The extra details are added behind the scenes when the buyer submits.

Can I see which RFQs came from a trade show?

Yes, with one small step. We give you a tagged link and QR code for your booth materials and follow-up emails, so buyers you met at IMTS, FABTECH, PACK EXPO or SupplySide show the show name on their RFQ. Without the tag they usually show up as a direct visit.

Who sees the details?

Whoever gets your RFQs today: the quotes inbox, your estimator, your sales manager, or the lead record in HubSpot, Salesforce, Zoho or Pipedrive. Nobody has to log into anything new.

Which form tools and CRMs does it work with?

HubSpot, WordPress forms (Gravity Forms, WPForms, Contact Form 7), Webflow, Wix, Squarespace, Typeform, Jotform and Unbounce. Details can be sent to HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive, Zoho, GoHighLevel, or to your RFQ notification email. Most other tools, including custom quote forms, can be added.

Do I need to be technical?

No. You give us access to your website or Google Tag Manager, we handle the setup, and you can remove the access when we’re done.

Is this allowed under privacy laws?

It’s built to be privacy-friendly. We only record how someone got to your site and what they viewed on it, stored on your own domain, and nothing is shared with ad networks. We give you a short note to add to your privacy policy, and if you use a cookie consent banner, tracking can wait for consent.

What if a buyer blocks tracking?

You still get the RFQ, just with fewer details. In practice most RFQs come through with the full story.

Is there a monthly fee?

No. It’s a one-time setup fee, with no annual contract. Add-ons such as extra forms or a second website are optional and priced up front.

How long does setup take?

One to two business days after the call. You get a test RFQ in your inbox so you can see it working before we’re done.
