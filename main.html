FAQs

What is lead source tracking?

Lead source tracking records how each lead found your business, such as a Google search, a LinkedIn post, another website, a directory listing, an email or a paid ad, and saves it with their form submission. You can then see which marketing actually brings in customers.

How do I know where my website leads come from?

Add lead source tracking to your contact form. When a visitor submits it, the source that brought them (a Google search, LinkedIn post, referral site, directory, email, or ad), the pages they viewed, how many times they visited, and their device and location are recorded with the submission and sent to your CRM or email. That’s what we set up.

How is this different from Google Analytics?

Google Analytics shows totals, such as how many visitors came from LinkedIn this month. It doesn’t tell you which source brought the specific person who filled out your form. Lead source tracking attaches the source, pages read and visit history to each individual lead, in your CRM or inbox.

Do I need to be technical?

No. You give us access to your website or Google Tag Manager, we handle the setup, and you can remove the access when we’re done.

Will my contact form look different?

No. Visitors see the same form. The extra details are added behind the scenes when they submit.

Which form tools and CRMs does it work with?

HubSpot, WordPress forms (Gravity Forms, WPForms, Contact Form 7), Webflow, Wix, Squarespace, Typeform, Jotform and Unbounce. Details can be sent to HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive, Zoho, GoHighLevel, or to your notification email. Most other tools can be added.

Do I need to add UTM tags to my links?

No. Visits from Google search, other websites and most social posts are recognized automatically. For paid ads and email campaigns, UTM tags add the campaign name and, for ads, the keyword. We show you how to tag them.

Is this allowed under privacy laws?

It’s built to be privacy-friendly. We only record how someone got to your site and what they viewed on it, stored on your own domain, and nothing is shared with ad networks. We give you a short note to add to your privacy policy, and if you use a cookie consent banner, tracking can wait for consent.

What if a visitor blocks tracking?

You still get the lead, just with fewer details. In practice most leads come through with the full story.

Is there a monthly fee?

No. It’s a one-time setup fee. Add-ons such as extra forms or a second website are optional and priced up front.

How long does setup take?

One to two business days after the call. You get a test lead in your inbox so you can see it working before we’re done.
